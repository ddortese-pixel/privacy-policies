Privacy Policy — Legacy Circle

Last updated: October 3, 2026
Contact: dixson2424@gmail.com

Plain-language summary

Legacy Circle is a learning app for kids ages 2-14, managed by a parent or guardian. We collect the minimum needed to run the learning experience, show no ads, use no third-party trackers, and never sell data.

Data we collect

  • Guardian account: name and email, so a grown-up owns the account.
  • Learner profiles: first name or nickname, age band, and learning progress (quiz scores, missions completed) — used only to personalize learning and show the guardian dashboard.
  • Device/usage basics: app version and crash logs to fix bugs.

What we NEVER do

  • No advertising of any kind, and no third-party ad trackers or analytics SDKs.
  • No chat or messaging between children.
  • We do not sell or share children's data with anyone, for any reason.

Guardian control

Guardians create and delete learner profiles at any time in the app. To request a copy or deletion of any data, email dixson2424@gmail.com — we respond to every request.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, guardians will be notified in-app before the change takes effect.
