Privacy Policy — OurSpace

Last updated: October 3, 2026
Contact: dixson2424@gmail.com

Plain-language summary

OurSpace is a social platform. To run it, we store what you give us: your account info, your posts, your messages, and media you upload. We do not sell your personal data. Ever.

Data we collect

  • Account data: name/handle, email, profile photo and bio you provide.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, reels, stories, group and room messages, voice/video call metadata needed to connect calls.
  • Device/usage basics: app version, device type, and crash logs so we can fix bugs. No advertising trackers.

How we use it

Only to operate and improve OurSpace: showing your feed, delivering messages, moderating abuse, and keeping the service running.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Content you post publicly is visible to other users by design. We disclose data only if required by law.

Your control

You can edit or delete your posts and profile at any time from the app. To request a copy or deletion of your account data, email dixson2424@gmail.com.

Children

OurSpace is a general-audience social platform and is not directed at children under 13. Family Link and Parental controls are provided for guardian-supervised use.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will announce it in the app's Bulletin Board.
