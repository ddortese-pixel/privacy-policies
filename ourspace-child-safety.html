Child Safety Standards — OurSpace

Last updated: October 29, 2026
Designated child safety contact: dixson2424@gmail.com

Our commitment

OurSpace has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards are the rules we publish, enforce, and hold ourselves to. OurSpace is a social platform for users aged 13 and older. Accounts are not offered to children under 13, and our signup includes an age gate based on date of birth.

Prohibited content and behavior

  • Any content that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers a minor, in any form.
  • Any attempt to groom, solicit, or arrange contact with a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sexual content involving or directed at minors, including in posts, comments, messages, images, or video.
  • Sharing or requesting personal information of a minor for harmful purposes.

These prohibitions apply everywhere on OurSpace: profiles, posts, comments, stories, messages, and uploaded media.

Reporting in the app

Users can report posts, comments, and user profiles directly inside OurSpace, and can block other users. Reports go to our moderation review queue, where a person reviews them. Anyone can also report a child safety concern at any time by emailing our designated contact, dixson2424@gmail.com.

How we respond

  • We review reports of child safety concerns as a priority.
  • Content that violates these standards is removed, and accounts that post or solicit it are banned.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and cooperate with law enforcement and relevant regional and national authorities, as required by law.

Compliance

OurSpace complies with all relevant child safety laws. We keep these standards published and current, and we will update this page when our practices change.
